BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Your feedback helps. Changes aren’t guaranteed.

Not every piece of feedback will be used or result in a change. Suggestions will be considered alongside testing, overall vehicle balance and other drivers’ experiences. A Buff, Nerf or Keep vote is a recommendation, not a promise or a majority-rule decision.

Please keep feedback constructive and specific. Include your setup, conditions and what you experienced. All submissions are public; do not include private information. Driver experience is self-reported, and every experience level is welcome.
